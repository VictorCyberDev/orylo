# Questions for eGain on licensing, entitlements and permitted use

**Status / evidence level:** Questions only. No answers are recorded or assumed. Public information consulted on 2026-11-10: the hackathon page (https://www.egain.com/ai-agent-hackathon/), which says entrants may "Use an existing eGain account or create a trial account with a working email address" and lists no specific trial entitlements, no terms on IP, use of submissions or billing; and the AI Agent page (retrieved via https://www.egain.com/?p=30996), which defines a billable Resolution as "a conversation with a customer followed by 24 hours of silence." Product names such as Composer, Agentic Studio and Smart Evaluator appear on https://www.egain.com/products/ without description there. Orylo has no eGain credentials or tenant access at this time (see `docs/evidence/00-repository-baseline.md`).

## How to use this list

Send in writing to the hackathon organisers or the eGain contact they designate, and record the date, the person and the exact answer. Treat anything not in writing as unconfirmed. Keep tone cooperative: Orylo is exploring a complementary outcome layer and has no wish to misdescribe eGain's products.

## 1. Trial versus enterprise entitlements

1. Which product capabilities are available in a hackathon or standard trial account, and which require an enterprise or paid licence?
2. Is the trial time-limited? What happens to configured agents, workflows and data when it ends?
3. Are there limits on the number of agents, knowledge articles, conversations or users in a trial?
4. Can a trial account be upgraded without losing configuration?
5. Is a trial tenant allowed to be used for a public demo, and for how long?

## 2. Capability-specific questions

For each item below: Is it available in a trial? In an enterprise licence? Is it generally available, preview or by request? Are there separate fees?

6. **Agent actions:** Can an AI agent call external systems or perform actions, and what controls exist (approval, scoping, logging)?
7. **Pre-chat:** Can pre-chat data be captured and passed to the agent or to external systems?
8. **Native workflows:** What can they do, and what is the workflow library licence?
9. **Escalation:** What escalation targets and handoff payloads are supported? Can a case reference be passed with the handoff?
10. **Publishability:** What does "published and tested" require, and can a published agent be embedded on a third-party page, linked publicly, or kept private?
11. **Connectors:** Which connectors exist, which are in trial, and can a custom connector be built?
12. **APIs:** Which APIs are available (agent invocation, conversation transcripts, knowledge), what authentication is used, and which are enterprise-only?
13. **MCP:** Is there support for the Model Context Protocol, as client or server? Under what licence?
14. **A2A:** Is there support for agent-to-agent protocols? Under what licence?
15. **Composer:** What does it include, and what are the licensing and extensibility terms?
16. **Agentic Studio:** What does it include, and is it available to trial or hackathon users?
17. **Evaluator access (Smart Evaluator or equivalent):** Can reviewers or judges test an agent, and is evaluation tooling available to trial users?

## 3. Rate limits, quotas and reliability

18. What rate limits and quotas apply to APIs, conversations and model calls, in trial and in enterprise?
19. Are there published service levels, and do they apply to trial accounts?
20. Is there a sandbox separate from production?

## 4. Data, privacy and residency

21. Where is trial and enterprise data hosted? Can a region be chosen?
22. How long are transcripts and logs retained, and can they be exported or deleted on request?
23. Are customer conversations used to train or improve models, by eGain or any subprocessor?
24. Is synthetic-only data required for the hackathon, and are there rules for personal data in trial accounts?
25. What security attestations does eGain hold for the platform, and are they available under NDA? (Orylo holds none and will not imply any.)

## 5. Billing definitions

26. How exactly is a billable "Resolution" counted: when does the 24-hour period start, what restarts it, and what happens if the customer returns within 24 hours?
27. Are conversations handed to a person, abandoned, or answered by a fallback counted?
28. Are actions taken by an agent (for example calling an API) billed separately from conversations?
29. Are test and demo conversations billed?
30. Does eGain provide a way to report on resolutions that is independent of the customer's own systems? Can customers reconcile their bills against it?
31. Would eGain object to a partner measuring a different unit (a verified customer outcome) alongside eGain's metric, provided the two are clearly distinguished?

## 6. Partner, reseller and commercial terms

32. Is there a partner, technology-alliance, ISV or reseller programme? What are the eligibility requirements, fees and obligations?
33. Can a third party package an offering that depends on eGain, and under what branding rules?
34. Can a partner be listed or referenced publicly? What approval is needed?
35. Who owns integrations or workflows built by a partner on the platform?
36. Are there exclusivity or non-compete provisions?

## 7. Hackathon terms and use after the event

37. Who owns the IP in a hackathon submission? Does eGain receive any licence to it?
38. May eGain use, display or publish the submission, demo video or description, and for how long?
39. After the event, may the entrant continue to use, commercialise or open-source the work built, including workflows and configuration made in eGain tools?
40. May the entrant describe the project publicly as having been built with eGain, and under what wording?
41. What happens to the trial tenant and published agent after judging? Is there a grace period?
42. If a submission uses third-party code or assets, what disclosure is required (the page asks for disclosure of material pre-existing templates and assets)?
43. Are there conflict-of-interest rules if the entrant later seeks a commercial relationship with eGain?
44. Is the stated deadline (November 15, 11:59 PM PST; the page does not state the year) confirmed for 2026?
45. How will organisers access submissions for judging, and what data do they retain?
